# Problem 5: Field Levitation

### 1. Problem Statement

What electric field strength is required to make a proton levitate against Earth's gravity at the level of the Earth's surface? 
(Mass of proton $m_p \approx 1.67 \times 10^{-27} \text{ kg}$, charge of proton $e \approx 1.6 \times 10^{-19} \text{ C}$, gravitational acceleration $g \approx 9.8 \text{ m/s}^2$).

---

### 2. Solution and Explanation

**Concept Intuition:**
"Levitation" simply means that the object is not falling down. For an object to not fall down, something must be pushing it up equally as hard as gravity is pulling it down! 

Normally, objects are held up by the ground or a table (the Normal force). In this case, we want completely invisible magic: we want an **electric field** to push the proton upwards. Because a proton has a positive charge, it gets pushed in the exact same direction as the invisible electric field lines. Therefore, we know our electric field must point visibly **straight upward** to push the proton up against gravity.

#### Part A: Setting up the Balance

To achieve perfect levitation, the upward Electric Force ($F_e$) must perfectly cancel out the downward Gravitational Force ($F_g$):
$$F_e = F_g$$

The equation for the force exerted by an electric field $E$ on a charge $q$ is $F_e = qE$.
The equation for the gravitational force (weight) is $F_g = mg$.

Substituting these into our balance equation:
$$qE = mg$$

#### Part B: Solving for the Electric Field

We want to find precisely how strong the electric field ($E$) needs to be, so we algebraically isolate $E$:
$$E = \frac{mg}{q}$$

#### Part C: Calculating the Value

Now, we plug in the given values for our perfectly specific proton:
- $m_p \approx 1.67 \times 10^{-27} \text{ kg}$
- $g \approx 9.8 \text{ m/s}^2$
- $q \approx 1.6 \times 10^{-19} \text{ C}$

$$E = \frac{(1.67 \times 10^{-27})(9.8)}{1.6 \times 10^{-19}}$$
$$E = \frac{16.366 \times 10^{-27}}{1.6 \times 10^{-19}}$$
$$E \approx 10.23 \times 10^{-8} \text{ N/C}$$
$$E \approx 1.02 \times 10^{-7} \text{ N/C}$$

This is an incredibly tiny electric field! Because the proton's mass is so incomprehensibly small, gravity has almost zero effect on it. It requires almost no electrical pushing power to hold it up.

---

### 3. Final Answers

- **Electric field magnitude ($E$):** $\approx 1.02 \times 10^{-7} \text{ N/C}$ (or $\text{V/m}$)
- **Direction:** Straight upward.

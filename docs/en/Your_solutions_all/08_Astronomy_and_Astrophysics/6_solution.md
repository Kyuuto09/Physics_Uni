# Problem 6: Solar Gravity

### 1. Problem Statement

Calculate the acceleration due to gravity on the surface of the Sun. By what factor would your weight increase if you could stand on its surface? (Sun's mass $M_S \approx 2 \times 10^{30}\text{ kg}$; Sun's radius $R_S \approx 6.96 \times 10^8\text{ m}$).

---

### 2. Solution and Explanation

**Concept Intuition:**
Your "weight" is simply the force of gravity pulling down on your body's mass. If you go to a more massive planet (or in this case, a star), gravity pulls harder, so you weigh more—even though your body hasn't changed at all! We can find exactly how much harder gravity pulls by using the same gravity equation from Problem 3.

#### Step 1: Calculate the Sun's Gravity
The formula to find the acceleration due to gravity ($g$) on the surface of a celestial body is:
$$g = \frac{G \cdot M_S}{R_S^2}$$

*(In words: The acceleration due to gravity is equal to the top half—which is the Gravitational constant multiplied by the Mass of the Sun—all divided by the bottom half, which is the square of the Sun's radius).*

Let's plug in our known values (they are already in meters and kilograms, so no conversions needed!):
*   $G = 6.674 \times 10^{-11}\text{ m}^3\text{/(kg}\cdot\text{s}^2)$
*   $M_S = 2 \times 10^{30}\text{ kg}$
*   $R_S = 6.96 \times 10^8\text{ m}$

$$g_{sun} = \frac{(6.674 \times 10^{-11}) \cdot (2 \times 10^{30})}{(6.96 \times 10^8)^2}$$

Calculate the top and bottom separately:
$$g_{sun} = \frac{1.3348 \times 10^{20}}{4.84416 \times 10^{17}}$$
$$g_{sun} \approx 275.5\text{ m/s}^2$$

#### Step 2: Calculate the Weight Factor
Your weight is your mass multiplied by gravity ($W = mg$). Since your mass stays the same, the factor by which your weight increases is simply the ratio of the Sun's gravity to the Earth's gravity ($9.81\text{ m/s}^2$).

$$\text{Factor} = \frac{g_{sun}}{g_{earth}}$$
$$\text{Factor} = \frac{275.5\text{ m/s}^2}{9.81\text{ m/s}^2}$$
$$\text{Factor} \approx 28.08$$

This means if you magically built a fireproof platform on the "surface" of the Sun, you would feel roughly **28 times heavier** than you do right now! If you weigh 150 pounds on Earth, you would feel like you weigh over 4,200 pounds on the Sun—your bones would likely snap under your own weight instantly!

---

### 3. Final Answer

*   **Gravity on the Sun:** $g_{sun} \approx 275.5\text{ m/s}^2$
*   **Weight Increase Factor:** $\approx 28$ times heavier

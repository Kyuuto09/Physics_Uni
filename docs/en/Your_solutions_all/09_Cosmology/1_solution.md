# Problem 1: Cosmic Chronology

### 1. Problem Statement

**Place the following events of the early universe in chronological order** (from earliest to latest): Recombination (formation of neutral atoms), Big Bang Nucleosynthesis, Inflation, Formation of first galaxies, Quark-gluon plasma era.

---

### 2. Solution and Explanation

**Concept Intuition:**
The history of the universe is fundamentally a story of expansion and cooling. The early universe started unimaginably hot and dense. As it expanded, the temperature dropped, which allowed progressively larger and more complex structures to "freeze out" and form.

Here is the timeline of those major milestones:

1.  **Inflation (approx. $10^{-36}$ seconds):**
    Almost immediately after the Big Bang, the universe underwent an unimaginably rapid, exponential expansion. This smoothed out the universe and set the initial conditions for everything that followed.

2.  **Quark-gluon plasma era (approx. $10^{-12}$ to $10^{-6}$ seconds):**
    The universe was still a blistering trillion degrees. It was far too hot for quarks to bind together into protons and neutrons. Instead, it existed as a incredibly dense "soup" of free-roaming quarks and gluons.

3.  **Big Bang Nucleosynthesis (approx. $3$ to $20$ minutes):**
    The universe had cooled enough for protons and neutrons to stick together. During this brief window, they fused to form the very first atomic nuclei—mostly Helium-4, along with trace amounts of Deuterium and Lithium. (It was still far too hot for electrons to join in, so these were just bare nuclei).

4.  **Recombination (approx. $380,000$ years):**
    The universe finally cooled down to about $3000\text{ K}$. At this temperature, electrons were captured by the bare nuclei to form the first stable, **neutral atoms** (mostly Hydrogen). Because the electrons were no longer flying around freely, photons of light could suddenly travel unimpeded. The universe became transparent, releasing the light we now see as the Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB).

5.  **Formation of first galaxies (approx. $400$ million years):**
    After a long period known as the "Dark Ages", gravity slowly pulled vast clouds of neutral hydrogen gas together. Eventually, these clouds collapsed under their own weight to ignite the very first stars, which grouped together to form the earliest galaxies.

---

### 3. Final Answer

**Chronological Order (Earliest to Latest):**
1.  Inflation
2.  Quark-gluon plasma era
3.  Big Bang Nucleosynthesis
4.  Recombination (formation of neutral atoms)
5.  Formation of first galaxies

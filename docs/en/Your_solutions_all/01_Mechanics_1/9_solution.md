# Problem 9: Momentum Comparison

### 1. Problem Statement

Which has greater momentum: a 2-gram fly flying at $10 \text{ m/s}$ or a 60-gram tennis ball moving at $1 \text{ m/s}$?

---

### 2. Solution and Explanation

**Concept Intuition:**
Momentum ($p$) is a measure of "mass in motion." It basically tells you how hard it is to stop an object. It relies equally on two things: how heavy the object is (mass) and how fast it is going (velocity). The formula is simple multiplication:
$$p = m \cdot v$$

#### Step 1: Convert to Standard Units

In physics, we always want to use standard SI units. Mass should be in kilograms (kg), not grams. There are 1,000 grams in a kilogram, so we divide by 1,000.

- **Fly mass:** $2 \text{ g} = 0.002 \text{ kg}$
- **Tennis ball mass:** $60 \text{ g} = 0.060 \text{ kg}$

#### Step 2: Calculate the Fly's Momentum

Multiply the fly's mass by its velocity:
$$p_{\text{fly}} = 0.002 \text{ kg} \cdot 10 \text{ m/s}$$
$$p_{\text{fly}} = 0.02 \text{ kg}\cdot\text{m/s}$$

#### Step 3: Calculate the Tennis Ball's Momentum

Multiply the ball's mass by its velocity:
$$p_{\text{ball}} = 0.060 \text{ kg} \cdot 1 \text{ m/s}$$
$$p_{\text{ball}} = 0.06 \text{ kg}\cdot\text{m/s}$$

#### Step 4: Compare

We compare the two results:
$$0.06 > 0.02$$
The tennis ball's momentum is three times larger than the fly's momentum.

---

### 3. Final Answer

The **60-gram tennis ball** has greater momentum ($0.06 \text{ kg}\cdot\text{m/s}$) compared to the fly ($0.02 \text{ kg}\cdot\text{m/s}$).

# Problem 8: EM Spectrum

### 1. Problem Statement

List the following types of electromagnetic radiation in order of increasing wavelength: Infrared, Ultraviolet, Microwaves, X-rays, Radio waves, Gamma rays.

---

### 2. Solution and Explanation

**Concept Intuition:**
The electromagnetic (EM) spectrum encompasses all types of electromagnetic radiation. All of these waves travel at the same speed (the speed of light, $c$), but they differ wildly in how much energy they carry. 

Energy, frequency, and wavelength are strictly linked:
- High energy = High frequency = **Short wavelength**
- Low energy = Low frequency = **Long wavelength**

To order these by *increasing wavelength*, we must start with the most energetic, highest-frequency waves (the shortest wavelengths) and end with the least energetic, lowest-frequency waves (the longest wavelengths).

Let's break down the categories from the list provided:
1. **Gamma rays:** Produced by nuclear reactions and supernovas. These pack the most energy and therefore have the absolute shortest wavelengths (often smaller than an atom).
2. **X-rays:** Very high energy, capable of passing through soft human tissue but blocked by bone. Their wavelengths are slightly longer than gamma rays.
3. **Ultraviolet (UV):** The high-energy rays from the sun that cause sunburns. Their wavelengths are just shorter than the violet end of the visible light spectrum.
4. *(Visible light would go here, separating UV and IR, but it wasn't listed in the prompt).*
5. **Infrared (IR):** Heat radiation. We feel this from a fire or a warm body. Its wavelength is just longer than the red end of the visible light spectrum.
6. **Microwaves:** Lower energy waves used for radar, Wi-Fi, and cooking food. Their wavelengths range from millimeters to about a meter.
7. **Radio waves:** The lowest energy waves on the spectrum, used for broadcasting AM/FM radio and television. Their wavelengths are massive, ranging from meters to hundreds of kilometers!

---

### 3. Final Answer

In order of **increasing wavelength** (shortest to longest):

1. **Gamma rays** (Shortest)
2. **X-rays**
3. **Ultraviolet**
4. **Infrared**
5. **Microwaves**
6. **Radio waves** (Longest)

			# Problem 7: Wavelength and Frequency

### 1. Problem Statement

The human eye is most sensitive to light with a wavelength of about $550 \text{ nm}$. What color does this correspond to in the visible spectrum? What is the frequency of this light?

---

### 2. Solution and Explanation

**Concept Intuition:**
Light is an electromagnetic wave. All electromagnetic waves traveling through a vacuum (and for all practical purposes, through air) travel at the exact same constant speed: the speed of light ($c$). 

Because the speed is locked, the wavelength ($\lambda$) and frequency ($f$) are inversely proportional. A longer wavelength means a lower frequency, and a shorter wavelength means a higher frequency. The human eye has evolved to be most sensitive to wavelengths right in the exact middle of the sun's visible output spectrum.

#### Part 1: Identifying the Color
The visible light spectrum ranges roughly from $400 \text{ nm}$ (violet/blue) to $700 \text{ nm}$ (red).
The wavelength $550 \text{ nm}$ sits perfectly in the middle of this spectrum. In terms of human perception, light at $550 \text{ nm}$ corresponds to a bright **Yellow-Green** color. *(This is why high-visibility safety vests and modern fire trucks are often painted neon yellow-green!)*

#### Part 2: Calculating the Frequency
First, we must convert the given wavelength from nanometers to standard SI meters so we can interact with the speed of light.
- Wavelength ($\lambda$): $550 \text{ nm} = 550 \times 10^{-9} \text{ m}$ (or $5.5 \times 10^{-7} \text{ m}$)
- Speed of light ($c$): $\approx 3.0 \times 10^8 \text{ m/s}$

The wave equation connects speed, wavelength, and frequency:
$$c = \lambda \cdot f$$

We rearrange this to solve for frequency ($f$):
$$f = \frac{c}{\lambda}$$

Substitute our values:
$$f = \frac{3.0 \times 10^8 \text{ m/s}}{550 \times 10^{-9} \text{ m}}$$
$$f = \frac{3.0}{550} \times 10^{17} \text{ Hz}$$
$$f \approx 0.005454... \times 10^{17} \text{ Hz}$$
$$f \approx 5.45 \times 10^{14} \text{ Hz}$$

*(This can also be written as $545 \text{ THz}$, or Terahertz)*

---

### 3. Final Answers

1. **Color:** Yellow-Green
2. **Frequency ($f$):** $5.45 \times 10^{14} \text{ Hz}$ (or $545 \text{ THz}$)

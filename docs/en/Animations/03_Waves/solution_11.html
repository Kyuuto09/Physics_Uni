About the Math

Superposition:

u = (A/r₁)sin(k r₁ - ωt)
+ (A/r₂)sin(k r₂ - ωt)

Constructive Interference: Bright bands where the path difference is a multiple of λ.

Destructive Interference: Dark lines where waves cancel perfectly to zero.

Adjusting the Slit distance or Wavelength will change the interference fringes projected on the right.

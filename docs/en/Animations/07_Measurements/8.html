Mass-Spring Experimental Simulator

Task: You need to experimentally determine the unknown spring constant \(k\). The mass is known to be exactly \(1.00\text{ kg}\).

1. Release the mass.
2. Use the stopwatch to time exactly 10 full oscillations.
3. Click "Stop" to automatically record the time to the table.
4. Repeat at least 5 times (ideally 10) to collect a robust dataset.

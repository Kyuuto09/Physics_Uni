Physics Context: Ohm's Law
A series of voltage (\(V\)) and current (\(I\)) measurements for a resistor are taken. Ohm's Law states \(V = IR\). If the voltmeter has a zero-offset error, the equation becomes \(V = IR + V_0\).

This engine performs real-time Least Squares Linear Regression to perfectly calculate the resistance (\(R\)), the offset (\(V_0\)), and their statistical uncertainties!

EM Wave Analysis

Visualizing orthogonal Electric and Magnetic fields traveling through space.

Wave Components
$\vec{E}$ (Y-axis)
$\vec{B}$ (Z-axis)
$\vec{v}$ (X-axis)
Controls
General Wave Equations:
$\vec{E}(x,t) = E_0 \sin(kx - \omega t) \hat{j}$
$\vec{B}(x,t) = B_0 \sin(kx - \omega t) \hat{k}$
Notice: The Electric Field, Magnetic Field, and the direction of propagation are all perfectly mutually perpendicular. $\vec{S} \propto \vec{E} \times \vec{B}$.

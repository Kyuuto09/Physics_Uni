Resistor Cube Visualizer

Interactive Physics

This cube consists of 12 identical 1Ω resistors.

Click on any two nodes (the circles) to connect the power supply. The equivalent resistance will calculate dynamically.

Drag the cube to rotate it and inspect the current flow. Brighter, thicker lines carry more current.

Known Math Configurations:

Opposite Corners: 5/6 Ω (≈ 0.833 Ω)
Face Diagonal: 3/4 Ω (0.750 Ω)
Adjacent Nodes: 7/12 Ω (≈ 0.583 Ω)
